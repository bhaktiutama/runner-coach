RunCo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · App: RunCo (Android, iOS, web) · Developer: Bhakti Utama

In short: your training plan, workouts, effort ratings and health answers are stored only on your device. Our server only knows the email address you use to sign in. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

1. Who we are

RunCo is a running training app made by Bhakti Utama, an individual developer based in Indonesia ("we", "us"). Contact: privacy@bhaktiutama.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

The following data is created and stored only in the app's local database on your device. It is never sent to our servers, analytics or any third party:

  • Your goal, race date, training days, current running volume and the plan generated from them
  • Completed workouts, distances, durations, pace, effort ratings (RPE), notes and tags
  • Answers to the health screening questions and reasons you give for pausing the plan
  • The name you enter in the app, your settings and reminder times

Because this data is not stored in the cloud, it is not synced between devices. If you lose or reset your device without a backup, it cannot be recovered. You can export a backup file at any time (Profile > Data & privacy > Back up to file). That file is saved where you choose, and we never receive it.

Usage events and error reports are off until you turn them on, either on the plan preview when you create your account or in Profile > Data & privacy, and you can turn them off again there at any time.

When you sign in with Google or Apple, that provider shares your email address with us under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your password.

If you buy Premium in a future version, the payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store. We receive only the status of your subscription, never your payment details.

4. How we use data

  • To provide sign-in and your account
  • To check Premium access
  • To measure and improve the app with anonymous usage statistics

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except the processors listed above, who act on our instructions.

5. Where data is processed

Our processors may store data on servers outside Indonesia, including in the European Union. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS.

6. Delete your account and data

In the app: Profile > Data & privacy > Delete account. This deletes your account on our server and all RunCo data on your device. You can export a backup first.

Without the app: email privacy@bhaktiutama.com with the subject "RunCo account deletion" from the email address you signed in with. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Anonymous usage events cannot be linked back to you, so they cannot be selected for deletion; they expire on their own (section 3).

7. Your rights

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) and similar laws, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent. Most data is already in your hands on your device. For data we hold (your email), contact us at the address above.

8. Children

RunCo is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Health notice

RunCo is a training guide, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. Health screening answers stay on your device.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
